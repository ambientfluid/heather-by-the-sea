What we do

Services

Three core services, performed with care and the highest-quality products.

Hair

Cuts & styling · Highlights · Color · Corrective coloring · Keratin Treatment

A highly trained cosmetologist with over 25 years of experience, ready to tackle all of your hair needs. Combine with another service for a truly unparalleled experience.

Waxing

Eyebrow wax, tint & lamination · Brazilian · Female & male body waxing

As a Master Waxing Specialist, there's no better place for high-quality waxing. Please mention sensitive skin, recent tanning, or use of AHAs, glycolic acid, Retin-A or Renova before your appointment.

Lashes

Lash tinting · Lash lifting

Unhappy with your lash game? A lash tint will amaze and enhance the beauty of your eyes — especially with blonde lashes. We also offer a Lash MD serum for purchase to promote healthy, full lashes.
